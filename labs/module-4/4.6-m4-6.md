# 4.6 — Policy that can say no

What did admission actually reject? Which runtime behavior remains unproven?

## Investigate

1. Prepare an isolated namespace in the local kind cluster and inspect its Pod Security Admission mode.
2. Submit a deliberately noncompliant Pod specification and capture the admission response.
3. Submit a compliant workload, confirm admission, and check that it becomes operational.

## Evidence target

Namespace policy configuration, rejected Pod-creation response, and admitted healthy workload evidence.

## Check your work

Use the `security` vet check for a running Podman container. Admission, scan, SBOM and trust claims need the separate artifact/evidence rubric.

A passing automated check covers the named observations only. Use [the evidence rubric](../../docs/evidence-rubric.md) for the rest of this lesson’s claim.

## If the result differs

Preserve the exact error, inspect the workload and its events, then change one variable at a time.
