# 4.4 — Scan, then decide

Why is your disposition proportionate to the risk and supported by evidence?

## Investigate

1. Identify the exact image digest and produce a vulnerability scan for it.
2. Select a finding and investigate severity, affected package, fix availability, and exposure.
3. Document the proposed action; if the artifact changes, rescan and recheck application behavior.

## Evidence target

Digest, scan output or structured report, triage rationale, and revalidation evidence when a change was made.

## Check your work

Use the `security` vet check for a running Podman container. Admission, scan, SBOM and trust claims need the separate artifact/evidence rubric.

A passing automated check covers the named observations only. Use [the evidence rubric](../../docs/evidence-rubric.md) for the rest of this lesson’s claim.

## If the result differs

Preserve the exact error, inspect the workload and its events, then change one variable at a time.
