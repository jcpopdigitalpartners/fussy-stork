# 4.5 — An SBOM makes dependencies visible

Which component surprised you, and how could it have entered the image?

## Investigate

1. Identify the image that was scanned and choose a machine-readable SBOM format.
2. Generate an SBOM and find a component you did not explicitly declare.
3. Trace its likely origin and associate the inventory with the matching image digest.

## Evidence target

SPDX or CycloneDX SBOM plus a short explanation of one discovered direct, transitive, or base-image component.

## Check your work

Use the `security` vet check for a running Podman container. Admission, scan, SBOM and trust claims need the separate artifact/evidence rubric.

A passing automated check covers the named observations only. Use [the evidence rubric](../../docs/evidence-rubric.md) for the rest of this lesson’s claim.

## If the result differs

Preserve the exact error, inspect the workload and its events, then change one variable at a time.
