# 3.8 — Checkpoint 3: Prove recovery

Could another engineer reproduce your failure and verify the recovery?

## Investigate

1. Assemble five artifacts: failure hypothesis, baseline, observation, recovery action, and independent functional verification.
2. Test one claim in your local environment. Capture the command, result, and any failure.
3. Compare the observation with the evidence target. Explain what remains unproven.

## Evidence target

An evidence-backed claim that identifies exactly which failure modes the lab did—and did not—test.

## Check your work

Capture an `api` vet report before a fault, then rerun with `--baseline` after recovery. Complete the recovery evidence rubric.

A passing automated check covers the named observations only. Use [the evidence rubric](../../docs/evidence-rubric.md) for the rest of this lesson’s claim.

## If the result differs

Add a client-side functional test, the restored record validation, and precise limits of the local cluster.
