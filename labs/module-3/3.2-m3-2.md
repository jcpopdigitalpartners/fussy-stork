# 3.2 — Give data a lifecycle of its own

If the database Pod moves, where is its data actually stored?

## Investigate

1. Trace the relationship: database container → mounted volume → claim → volume → underlying storage.
2. Test one claim in your local environment. Capture the command, result, and any failure.
3. Compare the observation with the evidence target. Explain what remains unproven.

## Evidence target

A claim may be rebound to a replacement workload, but local storage remains vulnerable to loss of its backing node.

## Check your work

Capture an `api` vet report before a fault, then rerun with `--baseline` after recovery. Complete the recovery evidence rubric.

A passing automated check covers the named observations only. Use [the evidence rubric](../../docs/evidence-rubric.md) for the rest of this lesson’s claim.

## If the result differs

Inspect the StorageClass, provisioner, access mode, reclaim policy, and physical backing. Bound is not the same as redundant.
