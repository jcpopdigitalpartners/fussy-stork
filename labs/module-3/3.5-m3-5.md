# 3.5 — Give requests time to finish

What happens to a request that is in flight when its Pod begins terminating?

## Investigate

1. Compare a short request, a long-running request, and an abrupt kill. Predict which ones can finish in the grace period.
2. Test one claim in your local environment. Capture the command, result, and any failure.
3. Compare the observation with the evidence target. Explain what remains unproven.

## Evidence target

SIGTERM handling, grace period, and endpoint removal coordinate imperfectly; existing connections may still need to finish.

## Check your work

Capture an `api` vet report before a fault, then rerun with `--baseline` after recovery. Complete the recovery evidence rubric.

A passing automated check covers the named observations only. Use [the evidence rubric](../../docs/evidence-rubric.md) for the rest of this lesson’s claim.

## If the result differs

Consider open connections and delayed convergence; inspect endpoint conditions and app signal handling.
