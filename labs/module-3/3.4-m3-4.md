# 3.4 — Healthy, ready and alive

When should Kubernetes remove a Pod from traffic instead of restarting it?

## Investigate

1. Predict behavior for three conditions: slow startup, temporary database failure, and unrecoverable deadlock.
2. Test one claim in your local environment. Capture the command, result, and any failure.
3. Compare the observation with the evidence target. Explain what remains unproven.

## Evidence target

Probe outcomes lead to different actions. Readiness failure and liveness failure are not interchangeable.

## Check your work

Capture an `api` vet report before a fault, then rerun with `--baseline` after recovery. Complete the recovery evidence rubric.

A passing automated check covers the named observations only. Use [the evidence rubric](../../docs/evidence-rubric.md) for the rest of this lesson’s claim.

## If the result differs

Readiness does not, by itself, restart the container. Examine probe type, events, and configured thresholds.
