# 3.6 — Deploy without creating an incident

How can a new release fail without taking the whole Service down?

## Investigate

1. Predict the rollout when new Pods stay unready. Observe the relationship between desired replicas, available replicas, and revision history.
2. Test one claim in your local environment. Capture the command, result, and any failure.
3. Compare the observation with the evidence target. Explain what remains unproven.

## Evidence target

New Pods gain eligibility only after readiness succeeds; rollback requires behavioral verification.

## Check your work

Capture an `api` vet report before a fault, then rerun with `--baseline` after recovery. Complete the recovery evidence rubric.

A passing automated check covers the named observations only. Use [the evidence rubric](../../docs/evidence-rubric.md) for the rest of this lesson’s claim.

## If the result differs

Rollbacks change the Deployment Pod template; they do not automatically reverse schema migrations or restore deleted data.
